# HEATWISE: risk what-if dashboard

A what-if risk analysis exercise for SPCE0019 Project Simulation (UCL Centre for Systems Engineering).

Open the live page: https://sbegucl.github.io/heatwise/

Groups mitigate the High and Medium risks on a London heat-resilience retrofit project. They assess the residual and secondary risks of each response and balance probability and financial impact against the budget and the deadline. A Monte Carlo simulation (4,000 runs) forecasts the chance of staying within budget and of finishing on time. Round 2 is a week-10 update: one risk occurs, two risks close and the heat outlook worsens.

- `index.html`: the dashboard. It is self-contained, runs entirely in the browser and sends no data anywhere. Saved scenarios stay in the viewer's own browser.
- `HEATWISE_Risk_WhatIf_Workbook.xlsx`: the companion workbook. It holds the risk register in the module template, an expected-value (EMV) view of the same model, a scenario log, a decision sheet, the Round 2 brief and the student tasks.
- `archive/v1/`: the earlier monitoring-and-control version.

Data: ECA&D daily observations, London Heathrow (STAID 1860), May–September 1979–2023. Klein Tank, A.M.G. et al. (2002), Int. J. Climatol., 22, 1441–1453. https://www.ecad.eu

Project costs, probabilities, response options and secondary risks are illustrative teaching assumptions.
